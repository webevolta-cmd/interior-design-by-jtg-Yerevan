## Portfolio imagery

All project imagery comes from the studio's own public posts (Instagram
cross-posts on Facebook / Threads, Nov 2025 – Sep 2026). "After" images are the
studio's **design visualisations**; "Before" images are the studio's own photos
of the existing space. The website labels them accordingly and never presents a
visualisation as a photograph of a completed interior.

On eight "before" photographs the studio had overlaid a caption box ("Before/Առաջ").
That caption graphic was removed (local inpainting of the caption rectangle only) so it
does not clash with the website's own Before/After labels. Nothing else in those
photographs was changed. One "before" image (textured bedroom) was a collage with the
visualisation inset, so it is not used as a before/after comparison.

Eight hero visualisations were upscaled to 4K with Higgsfield (ByteDance
upscaler) to render sharply on large/Retina screens. Upscaling adds resolution
only; composition, materials and furniture are unchanged.
